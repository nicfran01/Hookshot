# Blocker: T17a UI and overlay rendering: the gear-menu lighting change   (2026-11-13)

The UI flicker and the two overlay faults are fixed (`T17a-ui-rendering.md`). This report covers only the third class,
the scene after the gear menu, whose cause was not found in the task.

## What the task asked
- Step 4: the brightness before and after the gear menu at a fixed view; its cause from the passes of the two frames;
  whether it comes back after an area reload.
- Acceptance: the mean brightness after the menu within 3% of before.
- Scope 4: a blocker report for what cannot be fixed in the task.

## What was found
- **A repeatable route**, `games/raiders/scripts/gear_menu.txt`: the player stands still in the hub, with PLUS at 80 s and
  B at 92 s. The pair is the frame capture's mean luma over 65-74 s and 95-108 s, plus the 20x20 blocks that changed by
  more than 3 levels.
- **Eight runs** (`T17a-ui-rendering.md`, step 4):
  - −3.25% / 62 blocks (`gear1`);
  - +0.00% / 49 (`gear2`);
  - +44.65% / 144 (`gear3`, under an A/B switch that copied GPU-written vertex data);
  - −3.15% / 62 (`gear4`);
  - −0.14% / 6 (`gear6`, the copies off);
  - −0.09% / 3, −1.34% / 34 and −0.23% / 7 (`gear5`, `gear7`, `gear8`, the final build).
- **What changes when it happens** (verified by eye, `gear1` shots 2130 and 2850):
  - the sky's blocks do not move at all, so it is not exposure and not the camera;
  - the sunlit surfaces lose their warm direct light, as if in shadow.
- **The +44.65% picture is washed out whole.** That is your V7, but under a switch that can feed stale data (inferred).
- **It is intermittent and not tied to the T17a copies** (inferred from the eight runs): it appeared under two
  builds and not under four, the old behaviour among them.
- **Ruled out by code** (verified):
  - Ocarina's exposure and fog factors: `gpu/vk/vk_shader.cpp:946`, off unless the game is Ocarina.
  - Pipelines not ready: `0.0 draws left out` in every frame line of the runs.

## What was tried
- **`RX_VK_DUMP` (`t17a_dump1`).** Each step is a 320x180 thumbnail. A run that dumps turns off the queue-order waits
  (`sync_waits_on()` is false while `dump_on()`, `gpu/vk/vk_replay.cpp`). Not used for this class.
- **The area-reload check:** not made.

## Options
1. **A dump of the lit passes on a run that shows the change** (one session).
   - Use `RX_VK_DUMP_STEPS` to limit it to the lighting passes, at full size, before and after the menu.
   - Repeat the route until one run shows the change; about 1 in 3 did.
   - Compare which textures the lit passes sample: `dump-in` lines (texture ids, `/G` GPU-written): shadow map, light
     buffers.
   - Risk: the dump's changed wait mode may hide it.
2. **`RX_VK_FLASH_RECORD=<the lit programs>`** without dumping: their draws' uniform hashes and textures in the frames
   before and after the menu, in normal replay. Cheaper and keeps the wait mode, but it needs the lit programs' keys
   first (option 1's dump-in lines, or `RX_VK_PASS_TIMING=2`).
3. **Leave it to your check:** if the final build no longer shows it in your play (3 of 3 scripted pairs within 3%), note
   it and close.

## Recommendation
Option 3 first, as part of T17a's step 6 at the Deck. If you still see it, a follow-up task with option 2, then option
1.
